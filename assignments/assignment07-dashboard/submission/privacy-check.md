# Privacy Check

The data come from the prepared NHANES Health Equity classroom dataset (`examples/nhanes-equity/data/nhanes_equity_v6.csv`) and are used for classroom learning only, not for population inference.

The dashboard and `dashboard-preview.png` show only counts and mean BMI for each survey cycle, with at least 1,089 people per cycle in the submitted Low Income view, and the caption avoids causal and national claims.

I shared only the aggregated summary table, chart screenshots, and the rendered HTML with an AI tool, and it read only the column names and per-cycle row counts of the data file, never individual records.
