# Privacy Check

The data come from the prepared NHANES Health Equity classroom dataset (`examples/nhanes-equity/data/nhanes_equity_v6.csv`) and are used for classroom learning only, not for population inference.

The dashboard shows only counts and mean BMI for each survey cycle, with at least 1,089 people per cycle in the submitted Low Income view, and `dashboard-preview.png` shows only the chart and caption; the caption avoids causal and national claims.

I shared only aggregated summaries (the summary table, rows per cycle, and income-ratio ranges by group), chart screenshots, and the rendered HTML with an AI tool, never individual records.
